## OVERVIEW
Autodarts.io is primarily a DIY camera-based automatic scoring system for steel dart boards capable of accuracies of over 99%.

Since this is a DIY project, be prepared to work on the hardware and software yourself.

However, don't worry, we are constantly working on the documentation to get you started as quickly as possible.
Additionally, our community is quite large and helpful when it comes to assisting new players.

[Join the Autodarts Community Discord server](https://discord.gg/autodarts)

## REQUIREMENTS
Autodarts.io is designed to run on cost-effective or repurposed hardware. It is therefore not necessary to spend a lot of money on the backend hardware.
The software runs on most hardware, such as Jetson Nano, Raspberry Pi 4, Intel NUC, Thin Clients & MAC.

{{<hint type=info icon=gdoc_info_outline >}}
Due to USB bandwidth limitations, some Laptops cannot handle 3 webcams. Laptops with USB 3.2 & USB-C ports are more likely to work.
{{< /hint >}}

All you need is:

- A standard dartboard with a 360-degree LED ring light to eliminate shadows.
- Three USB cameras without lens distortion.
- A computing unit like a Jetson Nano, Raspberry Pi 4, Intel NUC or equivalent.
- A mounting solution to position the cameras around the board.
- A Screen connected to your computing unit or a tablet or a small laptop to open the Autodarts web page.

## GAME MODES
Autodarts offers a variety of exciting game modes, each with its own customisable in-game settings.
- **X01:** Classic dart game with variants like 301, 501, 701, DI/DO & Bull Off etc.
- **Cricket/Tactics:** A strategic game focusing on closing numbers 15-20 and the bullseye.
- **Bermuda:** Each round targets specific segments with increasing difficulty.
- **Shanghai:** Score points by hitting the required target.  Scoring single, double, and treble of the target grants an instant win.
- **Gotcha:** A race to reach a specific score, where reaching your opponent's score sends them back to the start
- **Around the Clock:** Players must hit each number on the board in sequence.
- **Round the World:** Similar to Around the Clock, but players focus on 1 number per turn.
- **Random Checkout:** Practice finishing combinations to improve finishing skills.
- **Count Up:** Accumulate the highest score within a set number of turns.
- **Segment Training:** Focus on hitting specific segments for targeted practice.
- **Bob's 27:** A training game focusing on hitting doubles under pressure.

## TOURNAMENTS
32-person 501 tournaments are run throughout the day with variations in leg requirements and player average.

## STATISTICS
Autodarts provides comprehensive statistics that track your performance with detailed insights, including average scores, finish percentages, and game history. The system records metrics for most game modes, helping players identify strengths and areas for improvement. 

Additionally, Autodarts offers heat maps to visually represent your dart-throwing patterns, enabling you to analyze accuracy and consistency. Whether you’re playing solo, with friends, or in a tournament, Autodarts keeps you informed with real-time data and historical records, empowering you to elevate your game.
